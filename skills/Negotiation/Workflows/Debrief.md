# Debrief

After a negotiation, from a transcript, a thread, or his account.

## Done looks like

- **Where it stands.** What was agreed, what's open, and whether each agreement was a commitment yes or a counterfeit one, with the line that shows it.
- **"That's right" vs "you're right."** Every instance, and what preceded it.
- **Black swans.** What surfaced, and what was hinted at but never pursued, quoted.
- **Moments that turned it.** Two or three, quoted, each with what worked or the move that would have worked better.
- **His type's blind spot.** Did it show up? Where?
- **Next move.** The single next message or question, drafted, if the negotiation is still live.

Quote the transcript. A debrief built on paraphrase hides the moments that matter.

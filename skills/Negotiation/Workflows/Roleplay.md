# Roleplay

He rehearses; I play the counterpart.

## Setup

Confirm the counterpart, their type, their real constraints, and one black swan he doesn't know about. I invent a plausible one if he has no guess, and reveal it only if he digs it out or at the end.

## In character

- Play them **realistically hard**: their type's habits, their incentives, the counterfeit yes, the "that's not fair." A pushover counterpart is worthless rehearsal.
- Reward good moves the way a real counterpart would. A good label gets a real answer. A good calibrated question gets information.
- Keep each turn the length a real person would say.

## Out of character

He can say "pause" at any point. When he does, and at the end, give him:

- The two moves that landed and the one that cost him the most, quoted.
- The black swan, and whether he found it.
- One line on his type's blind spot if it showed.

Then offer to run it again from the moment that cost him.

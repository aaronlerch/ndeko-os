# Review

He has a draft going into a negotiation: Slack, email, doc, or spoken talking points.

## Done looks like

A short readout, then a revised draft he can send:

1. **Verdict in one line.** Does this move the negotiation forward or give something away?
2. **What it gives away.** For example, his number before theirs, a concession without anything in return, a deadline he didn't need, or a "yes" that commits him.
3. **The moves it's missing.** A label where they're clearly feeling something, an accusation audit before a hard ask, a calibrated question where it currently states a demand.
4. **Every "why"**, with a what/how replacement.
5. **Tactics out of bounds** for this counterpart, per the profile's scope line.
6. **The revised draft.** Moves from this skill, sound from the `Writing` skill's channel profile, with `VoiceCheck` run on it. Show what changed and the reason. Never rewrite silently.

If the draft was dictated, fix the transcription artifacts without counting them as findings.

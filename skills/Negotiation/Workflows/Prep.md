# Prep

Before a negotiation, or while deciding how to approach one.

## Done looks like

A **one-sheet** (structure in `../Voss.md`) he can read in two minutes before the conversation, where:

- The counterpart is classified (outside party or not, per the profile) and their likely type is named, with the evidence for it.
- His goal and walk-away are written down, and marked as **internal only**.
- Every label, accusation-audit line, and calibrated question is written in words he would actually say, ready to use.
- The two or three likeliest **black swans** are named as hypotheses, each with the question that would test it.
- The first two minutes are scripted: how he opens, the accusation audit, and the first calibrated question. The rest is a map, not a script.
- If an Ackerman plan is in bounds, the four numbers are computed, the final one precise, with the non-monetary item named.

## Gather first

Pull what exists before asking him: the email or Slack thread, prior meeting notes (Granola via `mcpshim`), the contract or offer. Ask him only for what can't be found, usually his goal and his real walk-away. One question with both is better than two.

## Watch for

- **His type's blind spot**, from the profile, at the moment it shows up in the plan, not as a closing caveat.
- **A plan that leads with his number.** Restructure it to lead with questions, and say so.
